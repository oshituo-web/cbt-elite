Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Here's exactly how we handle your data.

Effective Date: 8 May 2026 Last Updated: 8 May 2026 Version: 1.0
This Privacy Policy explains how CBT Elite Learning Systems ("CBT Elite", "we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal data when you use our platform. We comply with the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR) 2019.

1. Who We Are

CBT Elite is an online examination preparation platform for Nigerian students. Our services are accessible at cbt-elite.vercel.app.

Data Controller: CBT Elite Learning Systems
Contact: cbtellite@gmail.com

2. What Data We Collect

Category Data Collected Purpose
Account Data Name, email address, username, profile photo (optional) Account creation and authentication
Academic Data Target exam, subject choices, intended school Personalising your study plan
Performance Data Quiz scores, session history, streak counts, accuracy rates, topic mastery Analytics, progress tracking, and AI recommendations
Payment Data Transaction references, subscription tier (processed by Paystack) Billing and subscription management
Device & Usage Data Browser type, IP address, session timestamps, pages visited Security, performance optimisation, and abuse prevention
Communications Emails or messages you send us, error reports submitted in the app Support and platform improvement

We do NOT collect: Payment card numbers (handled entirely by Paystack), government ID numbers, or biometric data.

3. How We Collect Your Data

  • Directly from you — when you register, complete your profile, or contact support.
  • Automatically — as you use the platform (session data, performance metrics).
  • Via Google OAuth — if you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email, and profile picture as permitted by your Google account settings.
  • Via Paystack — payment processing; we receive only a transaction reference, not your card details.

4. How We Use Your Data

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To personalise your exam preparation experience using AI.
  • To track your performance, streaks, and progress over time.
  • To process subscription payments and verify access.
  • To send important account notifications (not marketing spam).
  • To detect and prevent fraudulent or abusive activity.
  • To improve our platform features and content quality.

We will never sell your personal data to third parties.

5. AI & Data Processing

CBT Elite uses Google Gemini AI to generate practice questions and explanations. Your anonymised performance data (e.g., weak topics, accuracy rates) may be used to personalise AI-generated content. We do not send personally identifiable information (like your name or email) to AI services.

6. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We only share data with trusted third parties necessary to operate our platform:

PartnerPurposeData Shared
Supabase Database & authentication infrastructure All account and performance data (encrypted at rest)
Paystack Payment processing Email, transaction amount (card data never reaches us)
Google OAuth sign-in & AI services Email, name (for sign-in); anonymised prompts (for AI)
Vercel Web hosting & CDN Server logs including IP addresses

We may also disclose data if required by Nigerian law or a valid court order.

7. Data Security

We take data security seriously. Our measures include:

  • All data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS.
  • Database data is encrypted at rest via Supabase.
  • Row-Level Security (RLS) policies ensure users can only access their own data.
  • Authentication is handled via Supabase Auth with industry-standard JWT tokens.
  • Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text.

No system is perfectly secure. If you suspect a security breach, please contact us immediately at cbtellite@gmail.com.

8. Data Retention

  • Active accounts: Data is retained for as long as your account exists.
  • Deleted accounts: Personal data is permanently deleted within 30 days of a deletion request, except where retention is required by law (e.g., payment records for 7 years under Nigerian financial regulations).
  • Anonymised analytics data may be retained indefinitely for platform improvement.

9. Your Rights (NDPR)

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation, you have the following rights:

👁️

Right to Access

Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you.

✏️

Right to Rectification

Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.

🗑️

Right to Erasure

Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten").

⛔

Right to Object

Object to processing of your data for marketing purposes.

📦

Right to Portability

Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.

⏸️

Right to Restrict

Request restriction of processing in certain circumstances.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at cbtellite@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

10. Cookies & Local Storage

CBT Elite uses browser localStorage and sessionStorage (not third-party tracking cookies) to:

  • Save your session and authentication tokens.
  • Remember your theme preference (dark/light mode).
  • Cache exam progress locally to prevent data loss on poor connections.
  • Store notification preferences and tour completion status.

We do not use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking cookies.

11. Children's Privacy

CBT Elite is intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent. If you believe a child under 13 has registered without consent, contact us at cbtellite@gmail.com and we will delete the account promptly.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via email or an in-app notification at least 14 days before changes take effect. Your continued use of CBT Elite after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

13. Contact & Complaints

For any privacy-related concerns or to exercise your rights, contact us:

  • Email: cbtellite@gmail.com
  • Platform: cbt-elite.vercel.app

If you are unsatisfied with our response, you may contact the National Information Technology Development Agency (NITDA) which oversees NDPR compliance in Nigeria.
